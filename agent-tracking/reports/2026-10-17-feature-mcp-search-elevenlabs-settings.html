Follow-up: ignored settings refused

On request, a setting the chosen TTS model ignores is now a 422 instead of being stored and dropped: ElevenLabs voice_settings per model, and Sarvam temperature on bulbul:v4-flash. The retired eleven_monolingual_v1 / eleven_multilingual_v1 (400 unsupported_model from ElevenLabs) are rejected too. scripts/migrate_tts_settings.py --apply cleans stored rows.
